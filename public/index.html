A NOTE ON PRIVACY

Browsing, with a little more care.

Veil runs on your device and routes pages through a local proxy. It blocks private-network destinations and limits request size. It does not provide anonymity: websites can still identify your connection, and this app does not encrypt traffic beyond the destination’s own HTTPS.

Some websites may not work correctly because they block embedded browsing or rely on features this lightweight proxy does not support.
